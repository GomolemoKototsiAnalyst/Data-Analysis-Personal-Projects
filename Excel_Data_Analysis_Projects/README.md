### IMDB Movies Rating Dashboard built in Power BI:
This project analysis the movies ratings from  IMDB through the 1920s to the early 2000s. The dashboard does rates the movies made in the periods by how each generations movies rated in IMDB from that period. Interesting insight was movies rating started to decline after 2010. This could potentially imply that the rise of CGI in movies reduced the creativity or story writing by produces or the quality of movie writing reaached it's peak in 2010 and decreased rapidly from there onwards.
